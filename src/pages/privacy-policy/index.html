Privacy Policy

What atleanworld.com collects, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it.

Who we are

This policy explains how Atlean World handles personal data on atleanworld.com. The website is run by Neptune Silhouette Unipessoal Lda (“Atlean World”, “we”, “us”), Av. de França 140, 4760-104 Vila Nova de Famalicão, Portugal. We are the data controller for the data described here.

For anything about your data, write to careers@atleanworld.com.

Last updated: 17 November 2026.

What we collect

We only collect what you send us, plus the minimum needed to run the website safely.

  • When you apply for a job: your name, email, phone number, LinkedIn profile (optional), your CV and any answers you give in the form, together with the job you applied for and when you gave your consent.
  • When you contact us as an employer: your name, company, job title, email, phone number and what you tell us about the roles you need to fill.
  • When you email us: your email address and what you write.
  • When you visit the website: our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs (IP address, browser, pages requested and time) to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse.

Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • To consider you for jobs, including the one you applied for and similar ones: based on your consent, which you give when you tick the box in the apply form, and on steps you ask us to take before a possible employment contract.
  • To reply to employers and offer our services: based on steps you ask us to take before a contract, and on our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries.
  • To keep the website secure and working, for example blocking automated spam: based on our legitimate interest.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as keeping records the law requires.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Who we share your data with

If you apply for a job, we may share your CV and application details with the employer that has the vacancy, so they can assess you. We tell you before we put you forward for a role with a different employer.

We also use trusted service providers who process data for us, only on our instructions and under a data processing agreement:

  • Supabase, which stores the website’s job listings and handles the application form;
  • Google Cloud, which hosts our recruitment system (in the European Union, Belgium) where applications and CVs are stored;
  • Google Workspace, which receives the employer contact form and our email;
  • Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that forms are sent by a person and not a bot;
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the website.

Some photos on the job pages are loaded from Unsplash, so your browser connects to Unsplash to show them, and Unsplash receives your IP address.

Transfers outside the European Union

Some of these providers are based in, or have support teams in, countries outside the European Economic Area, such as the United States. When data leaves the EEA, it is protected by an adequacy decision of the European Commission (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep your data

  • Applications and CVs: for up to 2 years after your last contact with us, so we can consider you for other roles, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. If you are hired, the data becomes part of the hiring process with the employer.
  • Employer enquiries: for as long as we are in contact, and up to 2 years after the last contact, unless a contract requires longer.
  • Technical logs: for a short time, as set by our hosting provider, normally no more than a few weeks.

Cookies

The public website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and does not track you across other websites.

Cloudflare Turnstile may store small technical data in your browser while you fill in a form, only to tell people and bots apart. The admin area, used only by our staff, keeps a sign-in session in the browser.

If we ever add analytics or other optional cookies, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

Links and content from other websites

Articles and pages may link to, or show content from, other websites such as Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok or YouTube. Those websites have their own privacy policies and may collect data about you when you visit them or interact with their content.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data;
  • limit how we use it, or object to how we use it;
  • send your data to you or to another organisation in a common format.

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time, for example to stop us considering you for jobs. This does not affect what we did before. To use any of these rights, write to careers@atleanworld.com. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (cnpd.pt), or to the authority where you live or work.

How we protect your data

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access limited to the Atlean team members who need it for their work. CVs are stored in a private storage space, not on the public website.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or the law changes. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version. If a change affects how we use data you already gave us, we will tell you.
